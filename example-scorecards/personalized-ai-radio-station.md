    competitors could add persistent interest profiles and automated
    discovery as a feature. The pain point evidence is moderate but
    indirect: people want better podcast discovery and are interested in
    AI audio, but nobody is specifically asking for "always-on personalized
    radio." Key risk: you're building a product that's one feature update
